SuperPlayer for Apple devices

SuperPlayer is an independent, native player for user-provided Xtream Codes, Stalker/MAC portals, and M3U playlists. It runs on Apple TV, iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

The short version

Built by independent developer Alberto Montagnese, SuperPlayer focuses on making provider setup and live television navigation feel at home on Apple platforms. It includes Apple TV remote navigation, Family Sharing for eligible purchases, provider testing, favorites, search, and live channel groups.

Content policy: SuperPlayer provides no channels, media, playlists, or subscriptions. Users connect sources they are authorized to access.

Product details

  • Platforms: Apple TV, iPhone, iPad, and Mac
  • Connections: Xtream Codes, Stalker/Ministra with MAC address, M3U and M3U8
  • Category: Entertainment / video player
  • Availability: App Store, with free trial and in-app purchase options
  • Support: support@infoalerts.online
