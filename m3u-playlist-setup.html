Setup guide

Add an M3U playlist URL

Connect a remote M3U or M3U8 playlist supplied by your provider and browse its live channels in SuperPlayer.

Before you begin: SuperPlayer contains no channels. You are responsible for having permission to access every source in your playlist.

What you need

  • A complete remote playlist URL beginning with http:// or https://.
  • An active provider account if the URL includes credentials.
  • A network connection that can reach the playlist host.

Setup steps

  1. Open SuperPlayer and choose Add Provider.
  2. Select M3U playlist.
  3. Paste or enter the complete playlist URL.
  4. Name the provider so it is easy to recognize.
  5. Save and wait for SuperPlayer to parse and organize the channels.

If the playlist does not load

Invalid or empty playlist

Open the URL in a browser or ask the provider to confirm that it returns M3U text beginning with #EXTM3U. A login page, expired link, or empty response is not a playable playlist.

Download timed out

Large lists and slow servers can take longer. Try again on a stable connection. If the host cannot be reached elsewhere, the provider needs to restore or replace the URL.

Some channels do not play

That usually means those stream entries are offline, geo-restricted, or encoded in a format unavailable on the device. Compare multiple channels before reporting a player-wide issue.

Need support? Email support@infoalerts.online with your device, app version, playlist domain, and exact error. Do not send embedded credentials.
